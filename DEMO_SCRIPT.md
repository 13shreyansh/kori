# One bank. A different app for every life.

A two-minute recording made by a person using the production site, https://kori-wheat.vercel.app. All interactions below are real. No event fires without an action. The invented ledgers move with the real Helsinki date, so read the displayed dates while recording. The amounts below are derived from the reference ledgers and recomputed by the app.

Before recording, visit the unlinked `/reset?customer=all` to clear prior session actions. Use a 1440 × 900 browser viewport. The mobile app also works at 390 px. Credentials are printed on the login screen. Do not put a customer picker over the product.

The savings moves are working session ledger actions in this test environment, not instructions sent to a bank. Home-loan time selection explicitly states that no booking is sent.
